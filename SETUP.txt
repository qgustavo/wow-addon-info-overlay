WowQuickRef

1. Double-click WowQuickRef-Overlay.exe and leave it running.
   The first launch copies the addon into WoW AddOns. If it cannot
   find WoW, pick the _retail_ folder when asked.

2. Play WoW in borderless windowed.

F8 toggles the panel. In-game, the addon button opens Blood DK.

If Windows says the app is unrecognized: More info → Run anyway.
